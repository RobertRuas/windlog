The module follows the established NestJS + Prisma pattern: a single `weekly-timesheet.module.ts` registers `WeeklyTimesheetController`, `WeeklyTimesheetService`, and `PrismaService`. The controller exposes REST endpoints under `/api/v1/weekly-timesheets` (`GET`, `POST`, `PUT`, `DELETE`, plus `/:id/submit` and `/project/:projectId`) protected by `AuthGuard('jwt')`; all business logic lives in the service — controllers only map DTOs and user context. The service implements RBAC via `canManageTimesheet` (Team Leader of the project, HR, or ADMIN) and `canViewTimesheet` (creator or any technician mentioned in entries), performs soft deletes through `deletedAt`, and generates the 7 days of the ISO week on creation using `getMondayOfISOWeek` and `parseDateSafe` to avoid timezone drift. The Prisma schema defines three models (`WeeklyTimesheet`, `WeeklyTimesheetDay`, `WeeklyTimesheetEntry`) linked to `Project` and `User`, with `TimesheetStatus` enum (DRAFT/SUBMITTED/APPROVED) and corresponding `LogAction` entries. On the frontend, `src/pages/weekly-timesheet/` contains the list page (`WeeklyTimesheetPage.tsx`) and detail page with two tabs (`TimesheetSheet` read-only view and `TimesheetFormEditor` form). Data access is centralized in `src/services/weekly-timesheet.service.ts` which wraps axios calls; mutations are encapsulated in `hooks/useTimesheetMutations.ts` using TanStack Query with keys `['timesheets', ...]` for lists and `['timesheet', id]` for details, invalidating both on write.
